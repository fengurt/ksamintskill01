# Fewer handoffs improve delivery confidence

The sample team should keep one accountable owner from evidence review through release.

> Takeaway: one owner and two explicit gates reduce avoidable coordination work.

## Sample measures

- 17 reviews completed
- 4 open decisions
- 2 release gates

| Option | Ownership | Review gates | Fit |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Shared queue | Rotating | 4 | Weak |
| Named owner | Stable | 2 | Strong |
| External handoff | Split | 3 | Mixed |
